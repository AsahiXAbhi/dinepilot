Hosting

Two hosting paths, explained plainly.

The website is yours either way. Where it lives is a choice about address, domain and who pays for hosting.

Free Hosting

No hosting charge

Hosted using GitHub Pages, a free static hosting service. You get a free github.io website address, and there is no monthly hosting charge. If you own a custom domain, it can be connected to the same site.

  • Hosted using GitHub Pages
  • Free github.io website address
  • No monthly hosting charge
  • A custom domain can be connected if you own one

Premium Hosting

Paid separately

Paid hosting through a suitable provider, for a custom domain and a more polished address. Recurring domain and hosting costs are paid by you, and the price depends on the provider and plan you choose.

  • Paid hosting through a provider
  • Custom domain support
  • Recurring domain and hosting costs paid by the customer
  • Pricing depends on the provider and selected plan

What the $19 covers

Design and build of a 1–3 page website, digital menu, hours and location, contact buttons, mobile layout and one revision.

What it doesn't cover

Premium hosting, domain registration, third-party booking or AI service fees, and new content beyond the included revision.

Before you pay

We confirm the scope and the hosting route in writing first, so there are no surprise costs after the site is built.
