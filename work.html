Beira Alta

Portuguese restaurant · Design demonstration

Beira Alta was designed as a warm, editorial restaurant site: a menu that is easy to scan on a phone, hours and location placed up front, and a direct route from a first visit to a table. The layout leans on rich food photography and generous whitespace rather than effects.

The demonstration is a working static website. It is not a commissioned project, and it does not represent a real restaurant's results.

Inside the demo

  • Menu-first page structure
  • Hours, location and contact up front
  • Responsive on phone and desktop
  • Photography-led editorial layout

Design studies
