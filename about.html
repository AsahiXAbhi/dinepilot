Helping Restaurants Make Their Mark Online.

DinePilot focuses on practical, affordable digital work for independent restaurants and cafés — the kind of business where every table matters.

DinePilot is a small digital studio with one focus: independent restaurants and cafés.

Most restaurant owners don't need a sprawling marketing programme. They need a website that loads quickly, looks like the place they've worked hard to create, and answers the questions guests ask before they arrive — what's on the menu, when are you open, where are you, and how do I get in touch.

We keep our scope tight and our pricing visible. A website should be a practical asset: something you can point to from Instagram, print on a menu, or hand to a supplier. Not another subscription to manage.

Every project starts with a conversation about your restaurant. We'd rather ask good questions than assume what you need, and we'd rather build something simple and right than something large and vague.

01

Affordable website solutions

A clear setup price and a free hosting option, so a strong online presence doesn't require a big budget.

Modern, thoughtful design

Personalised support

You work directly with the person designing and building your site, from the first call to handover.

Our standard
“If a guest can't find the menu, the hours or the door, the design isn't finished.”

That's the test we build against: clarity first, decoration second. It's also why we keep the process short, label what's ready honestly, and explain every cost before work begins.

Let's build something that fits your restaurant.

No pitch decks, no long contracts. Tell us about your menu and your space, and we'll suggest a sensible starting point.
