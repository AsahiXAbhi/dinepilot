What happens next

Three simple steps.

  1. A short conversation

    You tell us about your restaurant, your menu and what's frustrating you online.

  2. Scope and price in writing

    We confirm the pages you need, the content we'll work from and the $19 setup price.

  3. Design, review, launch

    You review a preview, request your included revision, and we hand over a live site.

Helpful to have ready

  • Your menu, even a rough version
  • Opening hours and address
  • Three to six photos of the food or room
  • Whether you already own a domain

You don't need everything ready to start. A rough menu and a few phone photos are enough for a first conversation.
